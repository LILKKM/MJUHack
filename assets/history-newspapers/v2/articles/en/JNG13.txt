HANSEONG, March 1776 — New King Declares: 'I Am Prince Sado's Son'

King Yeongjo has died at Jipgyeongdang in Gyeonghuigung after a reign of 52 years. His grandson has succeeded him in a ceremony at the palace's Sungjeongmun gate. Immediately after his accession, the new king declared before the ministers: 'I am the son of Crown Prince Sado.' The words reportedly left senior officials pale.

Fourteen years ago, Sado died after being confined in a rice chest on the orders of his father, the late king. Yeongjo subsequently made his grandson the adopted son of Crown Prince Hyojang, who had died earlier. The new ruler's public affirmation of his birth father is therefore being read as a stern warning to those involved in the tragedy.

As royal grandson, he lived under threats from political opponents, with rumors even of assassins entering the palace. He continued his studies and waited. Ministers now speculate that he will restore his father's honor and punish those responsible for his death.

The new king has also reportedly pledged to respect his predecessor's wishes and avoid excessive retaliation. Court and country are watching what the new era begun at Gyeonghuigung will bring.
