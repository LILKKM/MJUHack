SEOUL, April 27, 1991 — Myongji Student Dies After Police Beating

A university student died yesterday after being beaten by police during a demonstration. Kang Kyung-dae, 19, a first-year economics student at Myongji University, collapsed after plainclothes arrest officers struck him with steel pipes and batons outside the university's main gate in Namgajwa-dong, Seodaemun. He died while being taken to hospital. His body was transferred to Severance Hospital in Sinchon at about 6 p.m.

Students said demonstrators had rallied on campus to demand the release of their detained student council president and greater university autonomy before attempting to move beyond the gate. Officers wearing white helmets and denim jackets, known as the 'White Skull' squad, pursued students onto campus. Witnesses said several officers caught Kang as he tried to escape over a wall and repeatedly struck his head and body.

Kang had entered university this spring. Classmates struggled to speak of the friend who had attended the entrance ceremony with them only two months earlier. As word spread, thousands of students and citizens gathered overnight outside the Severance mortuary, demanding punishment of those responsible and the squad's disbandment.

Police have opened an inquiry into possible excessive force. Opposition politicians called the death a killing by state authorities and demanded the interior minister's dismissal. Chalk marks left by students remain on the asphalt outside the gate.
