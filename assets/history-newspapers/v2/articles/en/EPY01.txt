HANSEONG, Autumn 1457 — Prince Geumseong Executed Over Restoration Plot

Prince Geumseong, King Sejong's sixth son and an uncle of the former king Danjong, has died after receiving a royal order to drink poison. He had been exiled to Sunheung in Gyeongsang Province after last year's attempt by Seong Sam-mun and others to restore Danjong. He was accused of joining the Sunheung magistrate Yi Bo-heum in recruiting local soldiers and scholars for another restoration effort.

The court says the plan was exposed while proclamations were being circulated. Sunheung has since been swept by a violent crackdown. Many people implicated in the plot have been executed, and the district itself is reportedly close to being abolished. The prince's ties with his elder brother, King Sejo, have been severed completely.

The repercussions have reached the former king in Yeongwol. Reports say that Danjong, demoted to Prince Nosan and living there in exile, has also lost his life. Uncle and nephew have thus met tragic ends in the same year.

People in the capital quietly praise Geumseong's loyalty. Some shamans speak of honoring the unjustly killed prince as a spirit. The court says it has punished treason severely. Repeated killings among the royal family's own relatives, however, have left public feeling deeply troubled.
