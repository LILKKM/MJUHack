WESTERN HANSEONG, 1764 — Prince Sado's Mother Laid to Rest at Old Palace Site

Royal Consort Yeongbin of the Yi family, the mother of Crown Prince Sado, died last summer. Her burial place has been chosen at the former Yeonhuigung palace grounds west of the capital. The detached palace was extensively rebuilt in King Sejong's reign for royal stays and later became Seojamsil, a royal mulberry plantation.

Yeongbin entered the palace as a court woman, won the king's favor and gave birth to the crown prince. Two years ago she suffered the tragedy of her son's death after his confinement in a rice chest. Court accounts say that before the incident she had reported the prince's conduct to the king for the sake of the state and royal house. The grief of a mother who lost her son can scarcely be imagined.

The king has deeply mourned her death. Praising her for setting aside private affection for the country, he bestowed the name Uiyeol, also given to her tomb. Residents bowed their heads as the funeral procession passed through Seodaemun.

Grounds once used by kings for rest and banquets now hold the grave of the mother of a prince who met a tragic end. Royal splendor and sorrow have settled on the same land. Those who pass will be reminded of the earlier calamity.
