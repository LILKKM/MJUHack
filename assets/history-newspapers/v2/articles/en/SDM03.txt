SHANGHAI, April 1919 — Korean Provisional Government Formed in Shanghai

The independence movement that erupted in Gyeongseong on March 1 has crossed the sea and given rise to a new government in Shanghai. Independence activists from different regions met in the city's French Concession for a provisional assembly session lasting from the night of April 10 into April 11. They adopted the name 'Republic of Korea' and proclaimed the establishment of its provisional government.

The first article of the provisional charter declares the Republic of Korea a democratic republic: a state belonging to its people rather than an emperor. The charter also guarantees equality regardless of sex, social standing or wealth, together with freedom of religion, speech and residence. Syngman Rhee, now in the United States, was chosen as prime minister, and heads of the government departments were appointed.

A young participant said the delegates had built a framework for the nation in response to the wishes of those calling for independence. Difficult work lies ahead. Separate efforts to establish governments at home and abroad must be brought together. Establishing contact with people in Korea and raising funds for independence are also urgent tasks.

Japanese authorities are reportedly watching developments in Shanghai closely. Koreans are looking to see what course this small government, begun in rented rooms in exile, will take.
