HANSEONG, Spring 1396 — Capital's First Wall-Building Phase Finished in 49 Days

The first phase of the great wall surrounding the new capital Hanyang has been completed. In the first lunar month, the government summoned more than 118,000 men from Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Gangwon and other provinces. In 49 days they established a defensive circuit linking Baegaksan, Naksan, Mongmyeoksan and Inwangsan. The laborers returned home before the farming season began.

Following the ridges of the four mountains, the wall uses earth on level ground and stone on the slopes. Construction was divided into 97 sections identified in the sequence of the Thousand Character Classic, with each assigned to a district. Names of the responsible districts were reportedly carved into stones so that future failures could be traced.

After establishing Gyeongbokgung and Jongmyo last year, the king now has a city wall as well. The new dynasty's capital is taking shape. Large gates are planned to the east, west, north and south, with smaller gates between them for people and goods. Their names are to reflect the Confucian virtues.

The hurried work in bitter cold reportedly injured or killed many laborers. The court plans another call-up in autumn to finish the remaining sections and gates. The new country's defenses are rising through the people's labor and sacrifice.
