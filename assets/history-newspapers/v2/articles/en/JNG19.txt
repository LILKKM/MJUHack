UNHYEONGUNG, HANSEONG, Spring 1866 — Young King Weds at Unhyeongung

The 15-year-old king's wedding has been celebrated at Unhyeongung. The daughter of Min Chi-rok of the Yeoheung Min family was chosen as queen. Before the ceremony she stayed at Norakdang to learn royal etiquette. The king then came in person to receive his bride.

Unhyeongung is the house where the king was born and raised before taking the throne. His father, Heungseon Daewongun, now conducts state affairs here as regent. After his son's accession, he greatly expanded the residence, building Noandang and Norakdang. Its power and scale are now compared to a royal palace, prompting the saying that Unhyeongung itself is the government.

The Daewongun reportedly played a decisive role in choosing the queen. Orphaned young, she has a weak immediate family power base. Observers interpret the choice as an effort to avoid the dominance of royal in-laws seen in earlier years. Her membership in the same clan as the Daewongun's wife is also said to have been considered.

Crowds gathered outside Unhyeongung to see the grand procession. Foreign ships appearing off the coast and the continuing execution of Catholics have nevertheless left the country unsettled. Attention is fixed on the future of the newly married king and his powerful father.
