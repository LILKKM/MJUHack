SEOUL, August 13, 1960 — Yun Posun Elected Fourth President

Democratic Party lawmaker Yun Posun has been elected the first president under the parliamentary constitution adopted after the April 19 Revolution. The lower and upper houses met in a joint National Assembly session yesterday and elected him by an overwhelming margin. He immediately took the oath of office and began work.

Under the new constitution, the president represents the country as head of state, while executive government belongs to a cabinet led by the prime minister. Yun must nominate a prime minister for parliamentary approval. Rival old and new factions within the Democratic Party are already competing over the choice.

Born in Asan, Yun studied at the University of Edinburgh. After liberation he served as mayor of Seoul and commerce and industry minister before opposing the Liberal Party government. His home in Anguk-dong has long been a meeting place for opposition figures discussing national affairs.

Citizens have high hopes for a government following the collapse of dictatorship. Economic hardship, post-revolution disorder and demands to punish those responsible for election fraud nevertheless present formidable tasks. The country is watching the course of the new president whose political life has centered on an old Anguk-dong hanok.
