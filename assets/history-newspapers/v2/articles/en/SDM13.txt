SEOUL, June 10, 1987 — Yonsei Student Critical After Tear-Gas Strike

A Yonsei University student remains unconscious after being struck in the head by a police tear-gas projectile during a protest outside the university's main gate in Sinchon. Lee Han-yeol, 20, a second-year business student, collapsed yesterday afternoon. He was taken immediately to Severance Hospital but has not regained consciousness and remains in critical condition.

Students said more than 1,000 demonstrators had gathered on campus ahead of today's national rally against the cover-up of Park Jong-chol's death under torture and the government's refusal to change the constitution. They confronted police while attempting to move beyond the gate. During the crackdown, a tear-gas projectile struck the back of Lee's head. The sight of him bleeding while supported by a fellow student shocked those present.

Police say they are investigating whether he was hit by a directly fired projectile. Students insist officers aimed at people. Public anger, already inflamed by Park's death in police custody in January, is expected to intensify.

The government has deployed large numbers of police for today's nationwide rallies. Students gathered outside the hospital overnight to pray for Lee's recovery. The smell of tear gas still hangs over Sinchon.
